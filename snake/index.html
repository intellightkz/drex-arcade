How it works

You steer your snake. Drex Max steered its own, one API call per move: for every step it got the board and a few facts about each way it could turn (does it crash, does it get closer to the apple, how many free cells are ahead, how much room is left to move in), and picked one. Most apples wins.

A decision per step, with odds you can act on. Steering, routing and picking the next action is what Drex does for agents. New accounts get $25 of credit.
